The idea

Students need more than a single grade calculation. PredictMyGrade brings academic progress and study planning together to help students understand where they stand and explore what to work towards next.

Product overview

  • Academic tracking across university, college and GCSE study.
  • Progress dashboards, grade targets and what-if planning.
  • Study goals, deadlines and planning tools.

Visit the product website for current features, or join the free waitlist to hear when your place is ready.

My contribution

I designed and built PredictMyGrade on my own — product design, the Django application, prediction models, billing, deployment and the test suites — and I run it in production.

Under the hood

  • Django + PostgreSQL application deployed on Render, with WhiteNoise static files, a strict Content Security Policy and Sentry error monitoring.
  • Grade forecasting with scikit-learn random forest models trained on features such as average so far, trend, grade spread, credits remaining and deadline load — a lighter model on the free plan and a richer one on Premium.
  • AI study coach on the Claude API, with a rule-based fallback whenever the model refuses, errors or isn’t configured, plus a fair-use cap.
  • Subscriptions through Lemon Squeezy hosted checkout, with HMAC-verified webhooks that ignore stale and out-of-order events so an old subscription expiring can never revoke a newer one.
  • Accounts via social sign-in and MFA (django-allauth), with data export, backup/restore and full account deletion.
  • Background work such as reminders and scheduled jobs through Celery.

Verification

Over a thousand Django tests run against PostgreSQL in CI, with payment, AI, OAuth and email providers mocked at the HTTP/SDK boundary. Playwright suites drive the real UI through settings, modules, GCSE and college tools, predictions, billing and free/Premium gating, and a visual sweep captures key screens at desktop and phone widths in both themes. Production guard-rails refuse to boot with mock billing or development login enabled.
